# Dataset card: ANES 2024 with synthetic respondents (Study 3, held-out)

Status: human pool built on 9 November 2026 (`scripts/build_anes_human_pool.py`, counts in
`results/S2_anes_pool_summary.json`); synthetic respondents to be generated with notebook
F03. Files: main data CSV of 19 May 2026, Redacted Open-Ends of 23 September 2025,
questionnaire of 8 August 2024 (SHA-256 in the summary file).

## Purpose

A real survey with a real closed-ended social measurement, contaminated by machine
respondents whose closed answers come from the same language model that writes their open
text. This answers the request for a survey outcome that is not a text statistic.

## Human records

* Source: ANES 2024 Time Series Study, full release (main data file in CSV; "Redacted
  Open-Ends" Excel file), electionstudies.org. Public, no registration.
* Unit: pre-election respondent. Of 5,521 respondents, 4,926 form the human pool. Excluded,
  in this order: 245 paper interviews (no open-ended questions), 56 with a missing
  thermometer, 236 without open-ended text, 57 with an ANES redaction tag, 1 with pasted
  chatbot output. Pool by mode: web 3,979, face-to-face 881, phone 66; median document
  length 51 words (web) and 99 words (interviewer-typed).
* Outcomes (closed-ended, scaled to [0, 1]): feeling thermometers for Kamala Harris
  (V241156), Donald Trump (V241157), the Democratic Party (V241166), the Republican Party
  (V241167). Missing codes in the release are -9 and -8 only (998/999 do not occur). Pool
  means 0.480, 0.392, 0.462, 0.431; SDs 0.378, 0.394, 0.331, 0.325.
* Detector input: the respondent's non-empty answers to the eight like/dislike questions,
  in fixed order, one per line (`d2vi.synthetic.respondent_document`). Candidates:
  V241110, V241112, V241114, V241116 (filters V241109-V241115); parties: V241170, V241172,
  V241174, V241176 (filters V241169-V241175).
* Design variables kept for description only: sample type and interview mode, pre-election
  weight. The estimand of the study is the mean over the human records of the pool (as in
  Studies 1-2), not the weighted ANES population mean.

## Machine records

* Synthetic respondents: persona drawn with replacement from the ANES respondents'
  background variables (age V241458x, sex V241550, race and ethnicity V241501x, education
  V241465x, census region V243007; in the "political" condition also party identification
  V241227x and ideology V241177).
* The model answers the four thermometers and the eight open-ended questions in one JSON
  reply (`d2vi.synthetic.prompt.RespondentPrompt`).
* Generators (open weights, ungated, Colab T4): Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct,
  Phi-3.5-mini-instruct. Conditions: persona {demographic, political} x style
  {plain, human-like}; 400 respondents per model and condition (4,800 in total).
* Sampling: temperature 0.9, top-p 0.95, at most 450 new tokens; one retry with a new seed
  when the reply does not parse; unparsed respondents are reported and excluded.

## Known risks and how they are handled

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| Formatting artefacts of the release: `[CHAR(10)]`/`[CHAR(13)]` line-break codes (2,337 occurrences in the eight items), space-padded cells | `clean_open_end` decodes and normalises them, for human and synthetic text alike |
| Interviewer probe markers `/AE/`, `/AO/` ("anything else?", CANDLIK/PTYLIK probes) followed by "no" in 63% of face-to-face answers would reveal the mode, and their absence the synthetic class | Markers removed and "nothing more" segments dropped by `clean_open_end`; none remain in the pool |
| `[REDACTED ...]` tags (names, places, family members) would separate the classes trivially | The 57 respondents with a tag are excluded |
| Machine text already in the 2024 web survey: one web respondent pasted formatted chatbot output (Markdown headings, "Here are some points commonly cited ...") into four of eight answers; none in interviewer-administered modes. Undisclosed use without formatting cannot be counted, so the human class is presumed, not verified | That respondent is excluded and reported as evidence that the problem is real; report detector flag rates by mode; sensitivity analysis on interviewer-administered respondents (947) |
| Interviewer-typed answers (face-to-face, phone) are longer and less edited than web answers | Report detector operating points by mode |
| Persona prompts reproduce stereotypes | Synthetic respondents are used only as contamination; no substantive claim is made from them |
| ANES terms of use | ANES files and human texts are not redistributed; the repository holds code only; synthetic respondents are released on Zenodo |
| Question wording | Compared with the web version of the 2024 questionnaire (CANDLIK pp. 42-47, THERMPRE pp. 59-66, PTYLIK pp. 66-70) on 9 Nov 2026; `ANES2024.wording_checked = True` |
